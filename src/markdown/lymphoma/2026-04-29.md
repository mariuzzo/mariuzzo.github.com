Late last night I had to go to the emergency room. I had been feeling dizzy and weak since 7:30 PM. I thought it was just a temporary thing, but it got worse and worse. I was afraid of fainting, so we went to a nearby hospital.

The doctors did some tests and didn't find anything wrong with me. They gave me some fluids and medication to help with general discomfort. Then they recommended that I see my primary care doctor.

Good thing, I was able to be there in company of my wife, mother and sister.

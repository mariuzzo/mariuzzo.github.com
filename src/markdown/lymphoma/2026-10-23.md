## Why they decided to stop on the chemotherapy and continue on immunotherapy only?

**K-GemOx** is my current treatment that consist on **immunotherapy** ("K" for Keytruda; Pembroluzimab) and **chemotherapy** ("Gem" for Gemcitabine and "Ox" for Oxaliplatine). The chemotherapy part has several toxic side effects one of them being toxicity on pancreas and related organs. The immunotherapy has much less side efects in comparison and also is the one that will help more in my situation. Therefore, the risks of taking a last dose of chemotherapy were higher than the benefits, because although my lipase and amilase levels are decreasing, they are still on the high side.

From now on, I will continue on immunotherapy each 21 days and I will be able to resume in-person activities.

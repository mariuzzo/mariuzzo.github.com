2nd cycle of K-GEMOX

Chapter 3
July 9, 2026

Here we are! Just a couple of days later than the expected 21-day cycle. Each cycle consists of 21 days. The first day is for the initial administration of the chemotherapy drugs. THhe first cycle started on June 15th, so the second cycle was expected to start on July 6th. However, my main doctor, was on vacation and the oncologist who was covering for her was not available on that day. So, the doctor appointment was rescheduled for July 8th and she set the chemotherapy for the next day (today) July 9th.

Today, we arrived at the hospital around 8:15 AM. And they started the pre-medication at 10:40 AM, everything went fine. I have a cousin that work as day hospital doctor and she was there to check on me. Also, we met two sisters, one of them was her first day of chemotherapy and the other was the companion. We expressed our trust in Jehovah, and we received support one from the other. By 3:00 PM I was discharged and we went home.

I'm feeling good, no serious side effects, just a little bit of fatigue, minor nausea, and it feels like the pseudo flu want to show up, but I think it will be fine. Mimi is my 24/7 caregiver and she is taking care of me. My mom sent me the best soup ever. And now I'm going to take a nap.

All these daily details speaks to me of Jehovah's care and support.

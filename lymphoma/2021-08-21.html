Blood from veins in the foot

Chapter 2
August 21, 2021

This morning, I had blood tests for my upcoming doctor appointment. As my veins are damaged I expected the phlebotomist to have a hard time finding a proper vein. After 15 minutes, with two phlebotomists looking for a vein they suggested arterial blood. My immediate reaction was: "No, no please" 🙅‍♂️. They examined my feet, and they found a vein in my right foot. First time having a blood sample extracted from a foot.

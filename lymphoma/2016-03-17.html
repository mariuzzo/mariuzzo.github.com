Surgery

Chapter 1
March 17, 2016

Today I went for surgery (Lymph node biopsy). The surgeon removed one of the swollen lymph nodes for testing. She recommended that I rest for 7 days and avoid sun exposure on that side for 2 months.

Fun facts: They gave me an anesthesia for children… and it knocked me out in less than 10 seconds. Later, when I tried to wake up, I fought for 30 minutes before I was able to clearly think and act.

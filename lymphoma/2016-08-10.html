7th chemo: Wednesday, August 10th, 2016

Chapter 1
August 10, 2016

Today, I completed the 7th out of 12 chemos. I started at 9:30AM and finished by approx. 2:00PM. Yup, the duration was more than 5 hours without interruption.

While getting the chemo, I met a young girl who also has Hodgkin. Her name was Altagracia. We had a somehow… curious small conversation:

— Altagracia: how many years do you have attending the hospital?

— Me: (years!?) I only have 3 months. And you?

— Altagracia: I got Hodgkin 3 years ago… I was in remission until 2 months ago (which means she got Hodgkin twice)

— Me: 😳…

— Altagracia: I'm guilty here, I didn't take care of myself. I haven't eaten healthy during the remission period. Also, I didn't sleep well, I was going to bed late at night regularly…

Indeed, the lesson was learned: pay attention to your body, do not let anything, anyone, push you in a way that doesn't respect your body, your life.

That reminds me that as someone who has a personal relationship with Jehovah God, the source of life, that if I really care about my spiritual relationship I have to honor Him by protecting my body, which is part of my life.

“With you is the source of life; >
> By your light we can see light.”

— Psalm 36:9.
